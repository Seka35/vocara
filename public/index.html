GOT QUESTIONS?

Frequently Asked Questions

Will my sound tattoo still scan over time?

Vocara's scanner is engineered with relative height-profile matching and correlation algorithms, designed to stay readable across standard camera angles, lighting conditions, and natural skin variations.

What happens if I change my phone or delete the app?

Your sound memory is safely backed up in the cloud associated with your unique Sound Code. Simply scan your tattoo with any device running Vocara to play it instantly.

Who can listen to my sound tattoo?

Anyone who scans your physical tattoo with the Vocara camera scanner can hear your audio memory, making it a truly interactive piece of living art!

How long is my sound memory stored?

With our Immortal Pass, your voice recording is safely stored for life with zero recurring annual fees. For Starter Pass plans, your first year of cloud storage is completely free, and then just $9.99/year to keep your sound memory alive forever.

Can I use any audio file?

Yes, you can record your voice directly on our platform or upload any existing audio file (MP3, WAV, M4A) up to 20 seconds long.

Do I need a specific tattoo artist?

Not at all. We provide a high-precision stencil that any professional tattoo artist can use. They just need to trace the pure black sound wave stencil provided in your download.
